# Roomwise Product Agent

Part of the Roomwise team (led by the Roomwise Orchestrator).

Searches **live Google Shopping listings** (via Apify) for furniture and decor and lists the best-rated priced options with price (USD), rating, review count, link and retrieval time.

- Example: "Find a Japandi jute rug"
- Only USD-priced listings are shown. If a search fails or finds nothing, it says so; nothing is substituted.
- Stock and discounts are not checked.

For a complete plan within a budget, chat with the Roomwise Orchestrator.
