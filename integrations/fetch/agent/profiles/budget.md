# Roomwise Budget Agent

Part of the Roomwise team (led by the Roomwise Orchestrator).

Picks the best-rated combination of live listings that fits your budget, **including estimated sales tax (9.375% unless you give yours) and delivery (USD 49 per retailer)**. It says plainly when a budget is not feasible and shows the cheapest combination found and the shortfall.

- Example: "A bed and a rug for $1,200 all-in"

Planning only: nothing is reserved or purchased.
