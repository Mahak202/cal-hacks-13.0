# Roomwise Finance Agent

Part of the Roomwise team (led by the Roomwise Orchestrator).

**Planning only.** Splits a furniture plan into one order per retailer with estimated subtotal, tax, delivery and total, and checks spending limits you set: a total cap, a per-item cap, and allowed stores.

- Example: "Split my plan by store, max $600 per item"

Nothing is charged, reserved or ordered. No payment details are requested or stored.
