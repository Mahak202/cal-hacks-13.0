# Roomwise Quotation Agent

Part of the Roomwise team (led by the Roomwise Orchestrator).

Produces the **itemized quote** for a furniture plan: each item with retailer, price in USD and link, then subtotal, estimated tax, estimated delivery, estimated total and whether it fits the budget.

- Example: "Quote a bed and two side tables, budget $1,500"

Estimates only; stock, discounts and exact shipping are not checked. Nothing is purchased.
