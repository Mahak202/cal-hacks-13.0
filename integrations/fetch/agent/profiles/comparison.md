# Roomwise Comparison Agent

Part of the Roomwise team (led by the Roomwise Orchestrator).

Compares the available live options for one item (price, rating, reviews, link) against the one in your plan, with numbered options you can choose from.

- Example: "Compare Japandi rugs"
